AI-Based Bulk Fruit Detection

Mini Project · Shaganaa S · 24UVA050 · 23VAIS09

Live Demo

Abstract

  • AI helps machines identify objects from images.
  • Fruit detection can be automated using computer vision.
  • Useful for farms, warehouses, supermarkets, and industries.
  • Reduces manual effort and improves accuracy.

Problem Statement

  • Manual fruit counting is time-consuming.
  • Large quantities of fruits are difficult to inspect manually.
  • Human errors may occur during counting and classification.
  • Need for a fast and automated solution.

Objectives

  • Detect multiple fruits in a single image.
  • Identify different fruit types.
  • Count the detected fruits automatically.
  • Provide fast and accurate results.
  • Reduce human involvement.
